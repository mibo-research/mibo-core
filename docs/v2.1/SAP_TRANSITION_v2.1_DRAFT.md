# Core v2.1 SAP transition — DRAFT

This addendum is prospective and not yet registered. It does not replace the
frozen [v1.0 SAP](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21936410) or
[v2.0 SAP](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22264635).

## Version-specific estimand and denominators

The proposed estimand concerns the distributions of outputs under three
prospectively frozen first-party API configurations in `ACI`/`CLOSED`, not all
four historical lineages or consumer UI behavior. Keep protocol version,
registration, exact model/profile/source hashes, wave, site, lineage, query
form, language, window and replication in every analysis key.

Intended initial counts are 720 ordinary / 840 calibration; a technical retry
is not an additional independent cell. Retain all intended rows and separate
valid capture, technical failure, unsubmitted and invalid administration.
Primary cells still need at least 8 valid observations of the target 10;
partial 5–7 cells remain descriptive under inherited rules. Never turn an
unresolved code, administrative invalidity or technical failure into a zero.

## Historical firewall and approved baseline decision (registration pending)

Do not pool v2.1 confirmatory estimates with v1.0/UI/paired/frozen/ASH or
four-lineage v2.0–v2.0.4 results. Do not recompute historical intended totals by
removing Perplexity or re-label old observations as v2.1. Cross-version or
common-lineage historical comparisons, if later permitted, need a separate
prospective analysis plan and explicit version/model/coding-change caveats.

The owner approved W03 as the new v2.1 STD baseline and exclusion of W02 from
the current primary comparison. W02 remains historical/exploratory evidence;
any future common-three-lineage exploratory bridge needs its own prospective
plan and material-profile/version caveats. This is not automatic cross-version
pooling or a causal update contrast. The policy is fixed locally, not yet public.

A W03 cell requires the inherited >=8 valid logical responses of target 10.
Insufficient cells remain insufficient; never substitute W04 or another wave
based on observed results. An undefined outcome, including all-empty URL sets,
does not become a defined baseline merely because technical n reaches eight.
Keep technical-cell eligibility and outcome estimability separate. No imputation
or automatic baseline replacement is allowed for either kind of insufficiency.
WA/WB calibration begins separately at W04 under the unchanged calendar; do
not use those calibration windows as interchangeable W03 STD observations.

The separately registered W03 URL-string analysis is currently bound to
four-lineage v2.0.1/960 requests, an 80-slot I03 subset and a W02 comparison.
It is not automatically a v2.1 plan. The owner selected the route of preparing
a separate prospective W03 analysis amendment, now present as a local v1.1
draft. It describes six I03 cells/60 intended slots and now explicitly excludes
W03-minus-W02 from the primary plan and retains no such leave-one-out contrast. See the
dependency review. No historical registration, raw data, private manifest or
freeze is modified; the new draft still needs review and public registration.

## Preserved hypotheses and outcome scales

Inherit the variable-specific Codebook, normalization, registered hypotheses,
effect threshold .15, FDR .05, sustained-change .10 and Phi .80 framework.
This preparation changes none of those thresholds. Register any necessary
three-lineage degrees-of-freedom/generalizability implementation explicitly;
do not claim a four-service universe from the smaller intended panel.

Anchor I03 remains citation adequacy 0–2 with external verification; I05 is
verified attributes/9 (0–1); I07 is the six-component sum (0–6); I09 is the
four-component sum (0–4). Derive only from resolved canonical components.
Technically valid refusal/safety/nonanswers may have defined component codes
and are not excluded merely for response status. H-held components cannot be
finalized from two agreeing models.

## Rotating judge pairs and validation

Source family determines the non-self pair, so pair and source are confounded.
Report per-source, language, item and judge/pair diagnostics and all
disagreements/holds. Do not pool rotating-pair kappa into one reliability claim,
silently average categorical/ordinal disagreements, or apply unregistered
severity corrections. Shared calibration diagnoses but does not eliminate bias.

Keep the 72 authored development cases distinct from the >=240 separately
reserved validation responses (12 items × 2 languages × 10). The proposed
three-source allocation is 80 per family, yielding 480 non-self ratings; no
sample IDs, disjointness check or actual qualification is complete.
The inherited validation benchmarks are Macro-F1 >=.80 for binary/nominal,
ordinal Krippendorff alpha >=.80 and extraction exact match >=.95. These are
not replaced by pairwise Cohen kappa or weighted kappa and are not declared
satisfied by inter-AI agreement. The amended AI-only qualification criteria,
reference-label qualification policy and undefined-result holds are now fixed
by [the approved specification](SCORING_QUALIFICATION_SPEC_v1.0.md). Operational
admission is not independent validity. Unreferenced AI codes remain auxiliary/
exploratory, H values remain externally held, and actual sample locking, content
review, reference verification and empirical metric computation are not complete.
No second-human recruitment is requested by this draft. AI qualification alone
does not block the deterministic URL primary analysis or separately gated collection.
